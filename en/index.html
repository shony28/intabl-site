A simple start

Four steps to your store

  1. 01

    Copy the template

    Make your own copy of the Google Sheet. The connection script is included.

  2. 02

    Make your catalog public

    Choose Share → Anyone with the link → Viewer. Keep only public product information in this spreadsheet.

  3. 03

    Connect your store

    Choose INTABL → Connect store and review the Google permissions. Confirm in the dashboard using the same Google account.

  4. 04

    Make it yours

    Add products and photos. Choose your country, storefront language, currency and delivery options. Share your store link.

Good to know

Is it really free?

Creating and using an Intabl store is free. You can publish your catalog and receive orders without a subscription.

Where do I edit products?

In goods, or additional sheets whose names start with goods. Use category for categories. Keep the sheet names and column headers unchanged.

Can I choose the currency?

Yes. Choose a supported currency in your dashboard, then enter prices in that currency. Changing the currency does not convert your prices.

Can customers pay online?

Intabl collects orders. Arrange payment and delivery directly with your customer; integrated online payment is not currently available.

Why does Google ask for permission?

The script needs access to the current spreadsheet, Drive metadata for matching photos, and a connection to Intabl. Review the requested access and continue only if you trust the template. You can use a separate Google account for your store.

Everything in one place

Edit products in Google Sheets. Use the seller dashboard to manage orders, change statuses, add your logo and store pages, and configure delivery.

Connect Telegram to receive new-order notifications. Customers can subscribe to order-status updates too.

Before you connect

Google will ask for permission

In your copy, choose INTABL → Connect store, then select your Google account. You may see these screens. These examples use Google's Ukrainian interface; labels depend on your Google account language.

  1. 1. Advanced

    The app has not completed Google's verification. Read the warning before continuing.

  2. 2. Go to Intabl…

    If you decide to proceed, use the link marked “unsafe”.

  3. 3. Review access

    Review the permissions. To enable all features, choose Select all → Continue.

You can use a separate Google account for your store. Click any screenshot to enlarge it. More about permissions.
